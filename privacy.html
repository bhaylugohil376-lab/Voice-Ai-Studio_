Privacy Policy

A plain-language overview of information that may be processed when using VoiceAI Studio.

Account information

If you create an account, the authentication service may process information needed to register you, sign you in, and manage account recovery.

Content you submit

Text, audio, files, and other inputs you submit may be sent to the configured service provider to deliver a requested feature. Do not submit sensitive information unless you are comfortable with that processing.

Usage and technical data

The site or its backend may process usage records, request status, and technical logs to operate and troubleshoot features.

Provider services

Some features depend on third-party providers. Their processing is subject to their own terms and privacy policies. Actual data handling depends on which integrations are enabled.

Security

API secrets should be kept in server-side environment settings, never in browser code. Protect your account credentials and recovery codes.

Questions

For questions, visit the Contact page. This policy is a general project summary and should be reviewed against the deployed services and applicable legal requirements.
