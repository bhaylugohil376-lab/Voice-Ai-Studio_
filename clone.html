VOICE AI STUDIO

Voice Cloning

Create a clone of your own voice or a voice you have explicit permission to use. This uses your connected ElevenLabs account and may require an eligible plan or credits.

1. Add a voice sample

Use a clean recording with one speaker, little background noise, and clear speech. Supported formats depend on the provider. Maximum upload size: 20 MB.

2. Use the cloned voice

After a successful clone, its voice ID will be shown here. Then open Text to Speech, choose “My ElevenLabs voices”, and refresh the voice list.
